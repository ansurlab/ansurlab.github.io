01

Generative AI & VHLA for Dexterous Manipulation

Enhancing generalized manipulation capabilities through Vision-Haptic-Language-Action Model, specifically focusing on Data & Sample Efficiency and Multi-Modal Sensor Fusion(Visual+Tactile+Kinesthetic) Representation for robust imitation learning.

02

Reinforcement Learning for High Dexterity

Achieving Human-Level Dexterity and Dynamical Physical Interaction by leveraging advanced Reinforcement Learning algorithms and Model to learn complex contact-rich tasks.

03

Real-Time System & Bio Medical Robot Applications

Designing Hard Real-Time Control Systems for precise actuation and advancing minimally invasive Medical Robotics.
